# Scene: inside the factory

**The idea it teaches:** the factory is a building where Deck runs your AI teams: Deck is part of the factory. Inside, each feature gets its own AI team, and the teams work at their own pace, on different features or projects. Every feature passes the same six steps, each step says who does it, and sometimes work is sent back a step.

**How it plays:** the scene pins to the screen when its top reaches the top of the viewport, and scroll drives it for one and a half screen heights (GSAP ScrollTrigger, `pin`, `scrub: 0.6`). Once the stations are built, each team's feature travels along its belt on its own, in a loop, at that team's pace, but only while the scene is on screen. Scrolling back before that hides the features again.

**Still version (reduced motion, or before the script loads):** the final frame, with every label, and the three features at different stations (blue at Review, violet at Build, rose at Plan), each with its station lit. The frame is fixed, so the page does not shift when the script loads.

**On phones:** the drawing shows the step numbers; the six steps appear as a numbered list under the drawing, and the three captions as a list after it.

**Rules:** AI agents verdigris. The agent owners wear their team's feature cap (blue cube, violet pyramid, rose cylinder), because each feature has its own owner; the AI Leader and the AI reviewers wear the plain cap. The Supervisor is drawn half amber and half verdigris: a person or an AI agent. Systems steel; Deck steel and graphite, as in the outside scene. Labels never sit on an object. No word "backlog", and no "lane" (Deck uses that word for something else).
